What It Does

Everything a personal trainer does —
powered by AI

A full-stack fitness intelligence system built on computer vision and machine learning.

🎯

Real-Time Pose Detection

Google MediaPipe tracks 33 body landmarks per frame at 42+ FPS, giving instant joint-angle analysis.

📊

Form Score 0–100

Every rep is scored across symmetry, range of motion, posture, and movement control with weighted criteria.

🔢

Automatic Rep Counting

State-machine logic detects full reps only — eliminating false positives from partial or paused movements.

🎙️

Voice Coaching

Contextual audio feedback prioritised: posture → symmetry → ROM → velocity → encouragement, with cooldown management.

🧠

ML Rep Quality

A Gradient Boosting model blends with rule-based scoring (60/40) for enhanced rep quality prediction at <5ms latency.

📹

Video Upload Analysis

Upload any exercise clip — the same CV pipeline analyses every frame and produces a downloadable annotated video.

🥗

Nutrition Calculator

Mifflin-St Jeor BMR → TDEE → personalised macro targets (protein, carbs, fat) based on your goal and activity level.

💊

Supplement Guide

Goal-based educational supplement information. Static, curated data — never generative, always with medical disclaimers.

📈

Workout History

Session analytics with Supabase-backed storage, trend charts, exercise distribution and calorie history per user.

Form Analysis

How the 0–100 score is computed

Every rep is evaluated on four biomechanical dimensions. The final score blends rule-based analysis (60%) with a trained Gradient Boosting ML model (40%) for reliable, interpretable feedback.

Reps scoring above 75 are classified as Clean. Below 75 are Bad — triggering targeted voice correction.
